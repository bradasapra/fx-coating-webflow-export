Industrial Coating Services

Epoxy Flooring, Surface Preparation, Protective Coatings, and Tank Linings for Facilities Across Southwestern Ontario

  1. Home
  2. Services

Every facility has different demands on its floors, walls, and containment structures. Chemical exposure in a wastewater plant is not the same challenge as thermal shock in a food processing facility or abrasion in a grain elevator. Cookie-cutter coating solutions fail because they ignore these differences.

FX Coating Ltd. provides four core service categories — each engineered to address a specific set of facility challenges. We match the right preparation methods, coating chemistries, and installation techniques to your environment, your operating conditions, and the regulatory standards that govern your industry.

Every project begins with a site assessment: we evaluate substrate condition, chemical exposure profiles, traffic patterns, thermal demands, and downtime constraints before recommending a system. The result is a coating installation that performs for 10 to 25 years — not one that starts failing within the first winter.

Epoxy Flooring Systems

Durable, seamless floor coatings for warehouses, manufacturing plants, food processing facilities, and commercial buildings. Systems include FX Epoxy, FX Quartz, FX Shield, FX Flakes, and FX Quartz Decorative — each engineered for different performance requirements.

Concrete Surface Preparation

SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 compliant substrate preparation using shot blasting, scarification, and diamond grinding. Proper surface profiles are the foundation of every coating system — without them, even the best products fail.

Protective Coatings

Wall coatings, floor coatings, steel coatings, and corrosion barriers for concrete and metal substrates. FX Wall Armor, FX Wall-Glaze, FX Steel-Shield, and fiberglass-reinforced linings protect against chemical attack, moisture, and mechanical damage.

Tank & Vessel Linings

Waterproofing, corrosion-resistant linings, and secondary containment for silos, grain bins, water tanks, and industrial vessels. FX Black Rubber, FX White Rubber, FX Cool Roof, and FX Steel-Shield protect structures from moisture, chemicals, and UV degradation.
