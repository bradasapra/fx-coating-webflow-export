Protective Coatings

Wall Coatings, Floor Coatings, Steel Coatings, and Corrosion Barriers for Concrete and Metal Substrates

Your Walls Are Porous. Your Steel Is Corroding. Your Surfaces Are Absorbing the Chemicals You Are Trying to Contain.

Uncoated concrete block, poured concrete walls, and bare steel structures are not designed to resist the chemical, biological, and moisture exposure found in processing environments. Concrete is porous — it absorbs chemicals, harbours bacteria, and deteriorates from the inside out when exposed to acids, caustics, or sustained moisture. Steel corrodes the moment its protective mill scale is compromised. And once these surfaces begin to fail, the damage accelerates.

In a food processing facility, porous walls become a sanitation liability — bacteria colonize the pore structure faster than wash-down chemicals can reach them. In a wastewater treatment plant, unprotected concrete corrodes under hydrogen sulphide attack. In a manufacturing facility, bare steel structural members rust under condensation, chemical fumes, and splash exposure.

FX Coating provides protective coating systems for every substrate and environment — from food-grade wall glazes to heavy-duty steel encapsulation. Each system is engineered to solve a specific protection challenge, applied over properly prepared surfaces, and built to provide 10 to 25 years of service life.

Wall Coating Systems

FX Wall Armor is a high-build, fiberglass-reinforced wall lining system for environments where walls face impact, chemical exposure, and aggressive wash-down. The fiberglass mat is embedded into the epoxy matrix to create a monolithic, non-porous surface that bridges cracks, resists impact from equipment and carts, and withstands concentrated chemical exposure. FX Wall Armor is specified for food processing walls, chemical handling areas, wash bays, and any environment where standard paint or thin-film coatings cannot survive.

FX Wall-Glaze is a smooth, high-gloss wall coating for sanitary environments where easy cleaning is the primary requirement. The non-porous, seamless surface eliminates the joints, grout lines, and texture that harbour bacteria on tile, concrete block, and painted drywall. FX Wall-Glaze is CFIA compliant and specified for food packaging areas, cold storage rooms, commercial kitchens, pharmaceutical clean rooms, and healthcare environments where surface hygiene is critical.

For complete room encapsulation in food processing and sanitary environments, FX Wall Armor or FX Wall-Glaze is paired with FX Cove base — a radiused floor-to-wall transition that eliminates the 90-degree corner where bacteria collect and cleaning equipment cannot reach. Combined with an FX Shield or FX Epoxy floor, this creates a fully encapsulated, seamless processing environment from floor to ceiling.

Steel Coating Systems

FX Steel-Shield is a high-build epoxy coating system engineered for structural steel, equipment, and metal substrates in corrosive environments. Applied over abrasive blast-cleaned steel (SSPC-SP6 Commercial Blast or SSPC-SP10 Near-White Blast), FX Steel-Shield creates a dense, chemically resistant barrier that prevents moisture and corrosive agents from reaching the steel surface.

FX Steel-Shield is specified for:

  • Structural steel in wastewater treatment facilities — exposed to hydrogen sulphide, moisture, and chemical fumes
  • Steel components in grain elevators and agricultural facilities — bins, legs, conveyors, and structural framing
  • Equipment and piping in manufacturing plants — chemical tanks, process equipment, and structural supports
  • Secondary containment structures — steel dike walls, tank saddles, and equipment pads

Floor Protection Systems

Protective floor coatings go beyond basic epoxy to address specific environmental challenges:

  • FX Shield — polyurethane concrete for the most demanding floor environments: thermal shock, steam cleaning, concentrated chemical exposure, USDA/CFIA food contact compliance
  • FX Epoxy — high-build epoxy for warehouses, manufacturing, and moderate chemical environments at a competitive price point
  • FX Quartz — quartz-broadcast epoxy for anti-slip performance in wet processing areas, loading docks, and commercial kitchens
  • FX Solid-Thane — polyaspartic coating for rapid cure requirements and UV-stable environments where colour retention matters

Every floor coating project begins with SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 compliant surface preparation — the foundation that determines whether the coating bonds permanently or fails prematurely.

Fiberglass Reinforcement

For environments where impact resistance, crack-bridging capability, and structural reinforcement are required, FX Coating embeds fiberglass reinforcing mat into the coating system. Fiberglass reinforcement transforms a coating from a surface film into a structural lining. The woven glass mat distributes impact forces across a wider area, bridges substrate cracks up to 3 mm wide, and adds tensile strength that prevents the coating from cracking under thermal movement or structural settlement.

Fiberglass-reinforced systems are standard on FX Wall Armor installations and are added to floor and tank lining systems where the substrate condition or environmental demands require additional structural performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a floor coating and a wall coating?

Floor coatings must withstand mechanical abuse — forklift traffic, impact, abrasion, and point loading — that wall coatings never face. Wall coatings must resist splash exposure, wash-down chemicals, humidity, and condensation while maintaining adhesion on a vertical surface where gravity works against the coating. FX Coating uses different systems for each: FX Epoxy, FX Quartz, and FX Shield for floors; FX Wall Armor and FX Wall-Glaze for walls. The chemistry, application technique, and thickness are all different because the demands are different.

How do protective coatings prevent corrosion on steel structures?

Protective coatings prevent corrosion by creating a physical barrier between the steel substrate and the corrosive environment — moisture, chemicals, salt, and atmospheric contaminants. FX Steel-Shield is a high-build epoxy system that encapsulates the steel surface after proper abrasive blast cleaning to SSPC-SP6 or SP10 standards. The coating blocks oxygen and moisture from reaching the steel surface, stopping the electrochemical reaction that causes rust.

Are FX protective coatings food-safe for processing environments?

Yes, several FX protective coating systems meet food safety requirements. FX Wall-Glaze is specifically formulated as a smooth, non-porous wall coating for food processing environments — easy to clean, resistant to wash-down chemicals, and compliant with CFIA hygiene requirements. FX Shield floor coatings are USDA and CFIA compliant for incidental food contact. FX Cove base systems create seamless floor-to-wall transitions that eliminate harbourage points for bacteria.

How long do industrial protective coatings last?

Service life depends on the system, the environment, and the quality of surface preparation. FX Wall Armor and FX Wall-Glaze wall coatings typically provide 10 to 15 years of service in processing environments with regular wash-down. FX Steel-Shield on properly prepared steel delivers 15 to 25 years of corrosion protection. Floor systems range from 10 years for standard FX Epoxy to 20-plus years for FX Shield. Proper surface preparation to SSPC standards is the primary factor determining service life.
