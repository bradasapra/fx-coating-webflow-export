Our Projects

Real Results Across Southwestern Ontario

Every facility presents a different set of challenges — chemical exposure, moisture migration, thermal cycling, regulatory requirements, and production schedules that leave no room for extended shutdowns. The projects below represent a cross-section of the problems FX Coating solves for facility managers, engineers, and operations teams across Southwestern Ontario.

Each project follows the same disciplined approach: assess the existing conditions, identify the failure mode, specify the right FX system for the environment, prepare the substrate to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards, and install a coating system engineered to last under real operating conditions.
