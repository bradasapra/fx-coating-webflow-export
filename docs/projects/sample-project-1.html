Schefter Poultry — Hygienic Wall Coating

Non-Porous Wall System Eliminates Bacteria Traps and Cuts Cleaning Time in Half

The Challenge

Poultry processing facilities operate under some of the strictest sanitation requirements in the food industry. At Schefter Poultry, the walls had become a persistent liability. The existing wall surfaces were hard to clean, prone to flaking, and riddled with crevices that provided spaces for bacteria to grow — making it costly and time-intensive to keep up to code.

Each cleaning cycle required significant effort. Staff had to scrub into every crack, corner, and joint where organic matter could accumulate. Despite these efforts, the wall surfaces continued to degrade. Paint flaked off, exposing porous substrate underneath. Moisture penetrated the wall material, accelerating deterioration and creating additional harboring points for microbial contamination.

With the possibility of an OMAFRA (Ontario Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs) audit looming, the stakes were high. A failed audit could mean production shutdowns, costly remediation, and reputational damage. Schefter Poultry needed a wall solution that would not just pass inspection — it needed to fundamentally change the hygiene equation.

The FX Solution

FX Coating assessed the facility and specified FX Wall Armour — a reinforced, non-porous wall coating system designed specifically for food processing environments where hygiene is non-negotiable.

The installation began with thorough surface preparation. Existing loose and flaking coatings were removed. The wall substrate was cleaned, degreased, and profiled to ensure maximum adhesion of the new system. Every crack, joint, and crevice was addressed before the coating system was applied.

FX Coating lined the entire wall with a non-porous reinforced coating that sealed off all cracks and crevices where bacteria could hide. The team then applied a specialized non-stick finish over the base layer. This dual-layer system created a seamless, hygienic wall surface that achieved three critical objectives:

  • Bacteria elimination: The non-porous surface leaves no crevices, joints, or pores where organic matter and bacteria can accumulate. Every square inch of wall is cleanable with a single wipe-down.
  • Structural durability: The reinforced coating strengthened the wall surface against the physical impacts, pressure washing, and chemical exposure that are routine in poultry processing. No more flaking, chipping, or delamination.
  • Reduced cleaning time: The non-stick finish means that organic residue, blood, fat, and processing byproducts release easily during wash-down. Cleaning time was cut in half compared to the previous wall surfaces.

The Results

The impact was immediate and measurable. Cleaning crews reported a 50 percent reduction in the time required to sanitize the coated wall areas. What previously demanded intensive scrubbing now required only standard wash-down procedures. The non-stick finish meant that residue did not bond to the wall — it rinsed clean.

From a regulatory perspective, the seamless, non-porous wall surface eliminated the bacterial harboring points that had been a persistent concern. The facility moved from a defensive posture — hoping to pass the next audit — to a proactive one, with wall surfaces that exceed OMAFRA sanitation requirements by design rather than by effort.

The FX Wall Armour system also delivered long-term cost savings beyond the cleaning labor reduction. By eliminating the cycle of wall deterioration, patching, and repainting, the facility eliminated recurring maintenance costs that had been accepted as unavoidable. The reinforced coating is engineered to withstand years of daily wash-down, chemical exposure, and physical contact without degradation.

"The difference is night and day. Our cleaning crew used to spend hours scrubbing walls and we were still worried about audit results. After FX Wall Armour, the walls rinse clean in a single pass. We are audit-ready every day, not just on inspection days."

— Mark Schefter, Operations Manager, Schefter Poultry
