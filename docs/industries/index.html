Industries We Serve

Engineered Coating Solutions for the Facilities That Keep Ontario Running

  1. Home
  2. Industries

Every facility has a different set of demands on its floors, walls, and structural surfaces. Chemical exposure in a wastewater plant is nothing like the thermal shock in a food processing washdown area. Forklift traffic on a manufacturing floor creates different stresses than moisture infiltration in a grain elevator. Cookie-cutter coatings fail because they ignore these differences.

FX Coating Ltd. serves four core industries across Southwestern Ontario. Each one gets a coating strategy built around its specific operating conditions, regulatory requirements, and performance expectations. We do not sell products off a colour chart — we engineer surface protection systems matched to the environment they must survive in.

Grain Elevators & Agriculture

Moisture protection, wall linings, and sanitary flooring for barns, silos, dairy parlors, and poultry operations.

Wastewater Treatment

Chemical-resistant linings, secondary containment, and structural coatings for treatment plants and pump stations.

Food Processing

CFIA-compliant flooring, wall systems, and coved transitions for meat, dairy, bakery, and beverage facilities.

Manufacturing

Heavy-duty floor protection, safety marking, chemical containment, and structural steel coatings for plants and warehouses.

Not Sure Which System Fits Your Facility?

Tell us about your operating conditions — chemical exposure, traffic type, temperature range, and regulatory requirements — and we will recommend the right coating system for your industry.
