Moisture, Corrosion, and Bacteria Are Destroying Your Agricultural Infrastructure

Agricultural facilities endure conditions that no standard paint or sealant can survive. Grain elevators face constant moisture infiltration through concrete and steel — water that carries chlorides, promotes carbonation, and accelerates the spalling that turns a sound structure into a liability. Dairy parlors and milking rooms see standing water, manure acids, and chemical sanitizers on every surface, every day. Poultry barns accumulate ammonia, organic waste, and moisture that attack walls and floors relentlessly. And through all of it, OMAFRA auditors expect clean, intact, sanitary surfaces that prove your operation meets Ontario's agricultural standards.

The cost of ignoring surface degradation in agricultural buildings is not just cosmetic. Spalling concrete in a grain elevator creates contamination pathways that compromise stored grain quality. Cracked, porous walls in a poultry barn harbour bacteria in crevices that cannot be reached by washdown — turning every cleaning cycle into a partial measure that still leaves pathogens behind. Corroded steel bins and structural members lose load-bearing capacity over time. The repair costs compound until the only option is a full structural rebuild that could have been prevented with proper surface protection years earlier.

Grain Elevator Waterproofing and Structural Protection

Grain elevators present a unique coating challenge: large concrete and steel surfaces exposed to moisture from multiple directions. Below-grade walls face hydrostatic pressure from surrounding soil. Above-grade walls and ceilings experience condensation from temperature differentials between stored grain and exterior conditions. Elevator legs, boot pits, and distribution floors see abrasive grain flow combined with moisture and dust accumulation.

FX Black Rubber is a spray-applied rubber membrane engineered for exactly these conditions. Applied at 40 to 80 mils thickness, it creates a seamless, monolithic waterproof barrier that bridges hairline cracks up to 1/16 inch and adheres to both concrete and steel substrates. Unlike sheet membranes that require seams and adhesives — creating potential failure points at every joint — FX Black Rubber forms a continuous envelope that stops moisture infiltration at the substrate level.

For structural steel protection on bins, legs, and support structures, FX Steel-Shield provides a high-build epoxy system that resists corrosion from moisture, grain dust, and agricultural chemicals. The system includes a zinc-rich primer for cathodic protection, an intermediate build coat, and a UV-stable topcoat — a three-layer approach that delivers 15 to 20 years of service life on exposed steel.

Dairy Parlors, Milking Rooms, and Bulk Tank Areas

Dairy operations demand surfaces that withstand constant water, manure acids (pH as low as 4.5), iodine-based teat dips, alkaline CIP chemicals, and the mechanical impact of hoof traffic and equipment movement. Floors must provide slip resistance when wet while still being smooth enough to clean effectively — a contradiction that eliminates most conventional coatings from consideration.

FX Shield polyurethane concrete delivers the answer: a 6 to 9 mm monolithic floor system that withstands thermal shock from hot washdown water, resists the full range of dairy chemicals, and provides a textured surface profile that maintains traction under wet conditions without creating the surface roughness that traps bacteria. FX Shield's moisture tolerance means it can be applied to concrete slabs with elevated moisture vapour transmission — a common condition in dairy facilities built on grade without effective vapour barriers.

For parlor walls and ceilings, FX Wall-Glaze creates a smooth, non-porous coating that sheds water, resists mold growth, and eliminates the surface texture of painted concrete block where bacteria colonize. Pair it with FX Cove at all floor-to-wall transitions to eliminate the 90-degree joints where moisture and organic material accumulate — creating a seamless, fully washdown-ready room encapsulation.

Poultry Barns and Livestock Housing

Poultry barns face an aggressive combination of ammonia off-gassing, high humidity, organic waste accumulation, and frequent washdown between flocks. Standard latex and alkyd paints on concrete block walls begin flaking within one to two years — and every flake creates a new crevice for bacterial colonization. The result is a facility that gets harder to clean over time, not easier.

FX Wall Armor solves this problem by installing a fiberglass-reinforced panel system directly over existing block, plywood, or deteriorated drywall. The panels create a structural, non-porous wall surface that cannot flake, chip, or develop the micro-cracks that harbour pathogens. A topcoat of FX Solid-Thane — a non-stick urethane finish — prevents organic material from bonding to the wall surface, reducing cleaning time by up to 50 percent compared to painted block.

This is not a theoretical improvement. At Schefter Poultry, FX Coating installed a complete FX Wall Armor system that sealed off the cracks and crevices where bacteria had been hiding, applied a non-stick finish that prevented buildup, and cut cleaning time in half — all while bringing the facility into full OMAFRA compliance. At Helps Poultry, the egg sorting room received a decorative flake broadcast system with wall-to-floor transitions designed for complete washdown capability, while the cooler room got a thermal-resistant epoxy/urethane system built to handle temperature cycling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What coating system stops moisture damage inside grain elevators?

FX Black Rubber is a spray-applied rubber membrane that seals concrete and steel surfaces inside grain elevators against moisture infiltration. Applied at 40 to 80 mils thickness, it bridges hairline cracks and creates a monolithic waterproof barrier that prevents water from reaching the substrate. For elevator legs, boot pits, and below-grade walls where hydrostatic pressure is present, FX Black Rubber provides long-term protection without requiring a dry substrate at application.

How do you make poultry barn walls easier to clean and OMAFRA audit-ready?

FX Wall Armor installs a non-porous fiberglass reinforced panel directly over existing concrete block, plywood, or drywall. The surface is then finished with FX Solid-Thane, a non-stick urethane topcoat that prevents organic material from bonding to the wall. The result is a seamless, washdown-ready surface with no cracks, joints, or crevices for bacteria to colonize. Cleaning time is typically reduced by 50 percent compared to painted block walls, and the system passes OMAFRA and CFIA inspection standards.

Can you coat dairy parlor floors to handle constant water and manure exposure?

Yes. Dairy parlor floors require a system that withstands standing water, manure acids, hoof traffic, and daily washdown with chemical sanitizers. FX Shield polyurethane concrete provides a 6 to 9 mm monolithic floor that resists all of these exposures. For parlor walls and ceilings, FX Wall-Glaze creates a smooth, non-porous coating that sheds moisture and resists mold growth. Coved transitions using FX Cove eliminate the 90-degree floor-wall joint where bacteria and moisture accumulate.

How long do agricultural coatings last before they need replacement?

Service life depends on the system and the environment. FX Black Rubber waterproofing membranes on grain elevator walls typically last 15 to 25 years. FX Shield polyurethane concrete floors in dairy and poultry operations have a 15 to 20 year service life under normal conditions. FX Wall Armor fiberglass wall panels are rated for 20-plus years. All service life estimates assume proper surface preparation at installation and routine cleaning.
