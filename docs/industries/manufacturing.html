Your Production Floor Takes Constant Abuse — and Bare Concrete Is Losing the Fight

Manufacturing floors endure a combination of mechanical, chemical, and thermal stresses that bare concrete was never designed to handle. Loaded forklifts running 16-hour shifts grind the surface aggregate into dust. Hydraulic fluid, coolant, and solvent spills penetrate the porous slab and weaken it from within. Impact from dropped tools, pallets, and raw materials chips and cracks the surface. Over time, the floor deteriorates into a maintenance liability: dusting concrete that contaminates product, cracked surfaces that catch forklift wheels, and oil-stained areas that create slip hazards for workers.

The secondary costs of an uncoated or failed manufacturing floor compound quickly. Dust contamination from degrading concrete affects product quality in precision manufacturing and electronics assembly. Slip incidents in oily or wet areas drive WSIB claims and lost-time injuries. Chemical spills that penetrate the slab create environmental liabilities. And a deteriorating floor signals to customers, auditors, and prospective employees that the operation does not invest in its infrastructure.

FX Coating installs floor, wall, and structural steel coating systems engineered for the specific demands of manufacturing environments — from light-duty warehouse floors to heavy industrial production facilities with chemical exposure, thermal cycling, and aggressive traffic patterns.

Floor Systems for Heavy Industrial Traffic

FX Epoxy is the workhorse floor system for manufacturing. Applied at 1.5 to 3 mm thickness over shot-blasted concrete, it creates a dense, abrasion-resistant surface that withstands the daily punishment of forklift traffic, pallet jack wheels, and foot traffic across shifts. The seamless, non-porous surface eliminates dusting, simplifies cleaning, and provides a professional appearance that reflects well on the operation.

FX Epoxy resists a broad range of industrial chemicals including hydraulic fluids, motor oils, coolants, mild acids, and cleaning solvents. For areas with heavier chemical exposure — battery charging stations, chemical mixing areas, or process zones with aggressive spill potential — FX Shield polyurethane concrete provides superior chemical resistance in a thicker, more durable build.

At Brant Aero, the airplane hangar floor faced recurring contamination from oil leaks that were impossible to clean and impossible to coat with standard products. FX Coating removed surface grease with hot water degreasing, shot blasted the floor to open the concrete, applied a specialized oil-tolerant primer for long-term adhesion, and installed a high-performance, oil-resistant FX Epoxy system. The result was a clean, safe, durable surface that now resists chipping and oil intrusion.

Anti-Slip and Safety Marking

Wet areas, oil-prone zones, and transition points between indoor and outdoor surfaces create slip hazards that standard smooth-finish epoxy does not address. FX Quartz solves this by broadcasting silica quartz aggregate into the epoxy system at full coverage, creating a textured surface that maintains traction under wet, oily, and contaminated conditions.

The quartz aggregate profile is selected based on the slip hazard level: finer aggregate for moderate-risk areas like aisles and walkways, coarser aggregate for high-risk zones around wash stations, loading docks, and machine coolant areas. FX Quartz can be applied as a full-floor system or in designated safety zones — around machinery, along pedestrian walkways, or in chemical storage areas — integrated with the broader floor coating for a unified, professional finish.

Safety line marking — pedestrian walkways, forklift lanes, hazard zones, equipment boundaries, and emergency egress routes — is applied as part of the floor system using contrasting epoxy colours that resist wear from traffic and cleaning. Properly marked floors meet Ontario Occupational Health and Safety Act requirements for workplace safety and support lean manufacturing visual management programs.

Chemical Containment and Spill Protection

Manufacturing operations that store or handle chemicals — lubricants, solvents, acids, caustics, fuel — require secondary containment that prevents spills from reaching soil and groundwater. FX Black Rubber spray-applied membrane creates a seamless containment liner in berms, sumps, and dyked areas that resists the specific chemicals stored. For containment floors that also bear vehicle traffic, FX Shield polyurethane concrete provides chemical resistance with the mechanical durability to handle forklift and cart traffic over the containment surface.

Structural Steel Protection

Structural steel in manufacturing buildings — columns, beams, trusses, mezzanine structures, pipe racks, and equipment supports — corrodes over time from humidity, chemical vapours, and incidental contact with process chemicals. Annual touch-up painting with single-coat alkyd systems is a maintenance treadmill that never catches up with corrosion progression.

FX Steel-Shield breaks this cycle with a three-coat system: zinc-rich primer for cathodic protection, high-build epoxy intermediate coat, and UV-stable chemical-resistant topcoat. Applied over surfaces prepared to SSPC-SP6 or SSPC-SP10, the system delivers 15 to 20 years of service life — eliminating the annual repaint cycle and the scaffolding, production disruption, and labour costs that come with it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What floor coating can handle heavy forklift traffic in a manufacturing plant?

FX Epoxy is a high-build epoxy floor system designed for heavy industrial traffic. Applied at 1.5 to 3 mm thickness, it resists the abrasion and impact of loaded forklifts, pallet jacks, and steel-wheeled carts. For environments with chemical exposure in addition to heavy traffic, FX Shield polyurethane concrete at 6 to 9 mm provides superior abrasion resistance combined with chemical and thermal shock protection. Both systems are applied over shot-blasted concrete prepared to SSPC-SP13 standards to ensure permanent adhesion under heavy loads.

How do you add anti-slip safety to a manufacturing floor?

FX Quartz incorporates a full broadcast of silica quartz aggregate into the epoxy system, creating a textured surface that provides consistent traction under wet, oily, or contaminated conditions. The quartz profile is selected to match the slip hazard — finer aggregate for moderate-risk areas, coarser aggregate for high-risk zones around wash stations, chemical storage, and machine coolant areas. The system can be applied in designated safety zones or across entire floor areas, and integrates with safety line marking for comprehensive workplace safety compliance.

Can you coat a manufacturing floor without shutting down the entire plant?

Yes. FX Coating routinely installs floor systems in operating manufacturing facilities using phased installation schedules. The floor area is divided into zones that are coated sequentially — production continues in unaffected zones while each section is prepared, coated, and cured. FX Epoxy achieves light foot traffic readiness in 12 to 16 hours and full traffic readiness in 24 to 48 hours. Weekend installations — starting Friday evening with full production readiness Monday morning — are a standard scheduling option for facilities that cannot afford any weekday downtime.

What coating protects structural steel in a manufacturing building?

FX Steel-Shield is a multi-coat epoxy system for structural steel protection. The system includes a zinc-rich primer for cathodic corrosion protection, a high-build epoxy intermediate coat, and a UV-stable topcoat. It is specified for columns, beams, trusses, mezzanine structures, pipe racks, and equipment supports in manufacturing plants. Surface preparation follows SSPC-SP6 or SSPC-SP10 depending on the exposure environment. The system provides 15 to 20 years of service life and eliminates the annual touch-up maintenance cycle of conventional alkyd paint systems.
