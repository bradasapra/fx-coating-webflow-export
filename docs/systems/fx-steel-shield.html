Corrosion Is Destroying Your Steel Assets. Every Month You Wait, the Damage Gets Worse.

Steel structures exposed to Southwestern Ontario's climate face relentless corrosion pressure. Freeze-thaw cycling, road salt carried on wind and vehicles, industrial chemical exposure, condensation inside unheated buildings, and UV degradation all attack unprotected or poorly protected steel. Grain bins lose wall thickness. Structural members weaken at connections. Equipment frames pit and flake. Storage tanks develop pinhole leaks. The cost of steel replacement is orders of magnitude higher than the cost of proper protective coating — but only if the coating system is engineered for the actual exposure conditions.

FX Steel-Shield is a multi-coat protective system combining high-build epoxy primers with corrosion-inhibiting pigments and UV-stable polyurethane topcoats. It is not a single-product paint application — it is a specified coating system designed to SSPC and NACE standards, with each layer serving a distinct function in the corrosion prevention strategy.

Multi-Layer Protection Strategy

Effective steel corrosion prevention requires a systems approach. A single coat of paint — regardless of quality — cannot provide the barrier properties, corrosion inhibition, and UV resistance needed for long-term service. FX Steel-Shield uses three functional layers:

  • Primer coat (3–5 mils): A high-build epoxy primer with active corrosion-inhibiting pigments. This layer bonds directly to the prepared steel surface, fills the anchor profile created by blast cleaning, and provides the primary barrier against moisture and corrosive agents reaching the steel substrate. For immersion service or aggressive environments, zinc-rich primers may be specified for cathodic protection.
  • Intermediate coat (3–5 mils): A high-build epoxy barrier coat that adds film thickness and provides additional chemical resistance. This layer bridges any holidays or thin spots in the primer and creates the bulk of the coating system's barrier properties.
  • Topcoat (2–4 mils): A UV-stable aliphatic polyurethane finish coat that protects the underlying epoxy layers from UV degradation, provides colour and gloss retention, and creates the system's first line of defense against weathering, chemical splash, and mechanical abrasion.

The total system builds to 10 to 15 mils dry film thickness — verified by non-destructive film thickness testing during and after application to ensure specification compliance at every point on every surface.

Surface Preparation: The Foundation of Performance

No coating system performs better than its surface preparation allows. FX Steel-Shield requires abrasive blast cleaning to SSPC-SP6 (Commercial Blast Cleaning) minimum, with SSPC-SP10 (Near-White Blast Cleaning) specified for immersion service, chemical exposure, or critical structural members. The blast profile must achieve 1.5 to 3.0 mils anchor pattern depth for proper mechanical adhesion.

All rust, mill scale, existing coating failures, oil, grease, and surface contaminants are removed before the first coat of primer is applied. Environmental conditions are monitored throughout the application: surface temperature must be at least 3 degrees Celsius above dew point, relative humidity must be below 85 percent, and ambient temperature must be within the product's application range.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of steel structures can FX Steel-Shield protect?

FX Steel-Shield is specified for structural steel, steel storage tanks, grain bins and elevators, pipe racks, equipment frames, steel roofing, stairways, railings, and any exposed steel substrate. It performs on carbon steel, galvanized steel, and previously coated steel surfaces that have been properly prepared.

How does FX Steel-Shield compare to simple primer and paint?

FX Steel-Shield is a multi-coat, spec-grade system engineered for long-term corrosion prevention — not a maintenance paint job. It uses high-build epoxy primers with active corrosion inhibitors, intermediate barrier coats, and UV-stable polyurethane topcoats. The total system builds to 10 to 15 mils and delivers 15 to 25 years of service compared to 3 to 5 years for standard industrial paint.

What surface preparation does FX Steel-Shield require?

FX Steel-Shield requires surface preparation to SSPC-SP6 (Commercial Blast Cleaning) minimum, with SSPC-SP10 (Near-White Blast Cleaning) specified for immersion service or aggressive chemical environments. All rust, mill scale, existing coating failures, and surface contaminants must be removed to expose clean steel with the proper anchor profile for coating adhesion.
