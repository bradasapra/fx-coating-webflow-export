Your Concrete Floor Is Deteriorating. FX Epoxy Stops It.

Bare concrete absorbs oils, chemicals, and moisture. It dusts under traffic. It stains permanently. And once surface degradation begins, it accelerates — cracking, spalling, and creating safety hazards that cost more to repair every month you wait. If your facility handles light to medium foot and vehicle traffic, you need a floor coating that seals the substrate, resists everyday chemical exposure, and cleans up without specialized equipment.

FX Epoxy is a two-component epoxy resin flooring system engineered for exactly this scenario. It bonds directly to properly prepared concrete to create a seamless, non-porous surface that resists oils, cleaning chemicals, mild acids, and water penetration. The result is a floor that looks professional, performs consistently, and costs significantly less to maintain over its service life than untreated concrete.

How FX Epoxy Works

The system starts with thorough substrate preparation to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards. Concrete surfaces are mechanically profiled using diamond grinding or shot blasting to achieve the proper CSP (Concrete Surface Profile) for optimal adhesion. Any cracks, joints, or deteriorated areas are repaired before coating application.

A penetrating epoxy primer is applied first, saturating the prepared concrete to establish a deep mechanical bond. The body coat follows — a high-solids, two-component epoxy formulation applied at 10 to 15 mils dry film thickness. Depending on facility requirements, a clear or pigmented topcoat can be added for additional chemical resistance, UV stability, or aesthetic preference. The entire system typically measures 15 to 25 mils total when complete.

Cure time is predictable: light foot traffic within 24 hours at 20 degrees Celsius, with full chemical and mechanical resistance developing at 72 hours. Full service readiness — including forklift traffic and cleaning — is achieved within 5 to 7 days.

Where FX Epoxy Performs Best

FX Epoxy is the right choice for facilities with light to medium traffic loads that need reliable, long-term surface protection without the cost of a heavy-duty urethane or polyurethane concrete system. Common installations include:

  • Warehouse floors and storage areas with pallet jack and light forklift traffic
  • Commercial kitchens and break rooms where spills need fast cleanup
  • Mechanical rooms, electrical rooms, and utility corridors
  • Retail showrooms and automotive dealership service areas
  • Office building common areas and parking structures
  • Agricultural equipment storage buildings and maintenance shops

For heavier demands — food processing wet areas, chemical manufacturing, or facilities with thermal shock from steam cleaning — consider FX Shield or FX Solid-Thane, which are engineered for those extreme conditions.

Why Facility Managers Choose FX Epoxy

Cost efficiency is the primary driver. FX Epoxy delivers genuine surface protection — chemical resistance, dust prevention, moisture sealing, and aesthetic improvement — at a lower price point than urethane or MMA systems. For facilities where extreme thermal cycling, heavy chemical exposure, or constant wet-process cleaning are not factors, this system provides excellent value without over-engineering the solution.

Maintenance is straightforward. The seamless, non-porous surface eliminates the grout lines, cracks, and pores where bacteria, mould, and contaminants accumulate on bare concrete. Routine cleaning with standard floor scrubbers and mild detergent keeps the surface performing and looking sharp for years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does FX Epoxy last on a commercial floor?

FX Epoxy typically delivers 7 to 15 years of service life depending on traffic levels, chemical exposure, and maintenance practices. In light traffic areas like offices and storage rooms, it can easily exceed 15 years with routine cleaning.

Can FX Epoxy be applied over existing concrete floors?

Yes. FX Epoxy is applied directly over properly prepared concrete substrates. Surface preparation to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards ensures a strong mechanical bond. Existing coatings, sealers, or contaminants must be removed before application.

What is the downtime for an FX Epoxy installation?

Most FX Epoxy installations can be completed in 2 to 3 days including surface preparation, primer, and topcoat. Light foot traffic is possible within 24 hours of the final coat, with full cure and heavy traffic readiness at 72 hours.
