FX Wall Armor — Reinforced Fiberglass Wall Protection

Seamless, hygienic, and impact-resistant epoxy wall coating for high-abuse environments where sanitation and durability are non-negotiable.

The Problem: Walls That Cannot Withstand Your Operations

In food processing plants, dairy facilities, meat packing operations, and agricultural buildings, walls take constant punishment. High-pressure wash-downs at 1,400+ PSI strip conventional paint. Forklift impacts crack drywall and concrete block. Chemical sanitizers penetrate porous surfaces, creating bacterial harboring points that fail CFIA and third-party audits. Moisture migrates through block walls, fostering mould growth behind finished surfaces. The result is a maintenance cycle that never ends — patching, repainting, and hoping the next inspection goes better than the last.

Standard paint systems, FRP panels, and even basic epoxy coatings cannot solve this problem. Paint lacks chemical resistance. FRP panels have seams that harbor bacteria and trap moisture. Basic epoxy coatings chip and delaminate under mechanical impact. These are band-aid solutions for facilities that demand permanent protection.

The Solution: FX Wall Armor

FX Wall Armor is a reinforced fiberglass mat epoxy system engineered specifically for high-abuse wall environments. Unlike surface-applied coatings that sit on top of the substrate, FX Wall Armor embeds a fiberglass reinforcement mat directly into the epoxy matrix, creating a monolithic barrier that is structurally bonded to the wall. The result is a seamless, non-porous, impact-resistant surface that withstands the daily reality of industrial operations — forklift contact, high-pressure cleaning, chemical exposure, and thermal cycling.

The system begins with proper surface preparation per SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards. The substrate is profiled, primed, and the fiberglass mat is embedded into a high-build epoxy base coat. A finish coat of chemical-resistant epoxy seals the system, creating a smooth, cleanable surface with no seams, joints, or fasteners where bacteria can hide. The entire assembly cures to a minimum thickness of 40 mils — roughly four times the thickness of a standard industrial paint system.

Why FX Wall Armor Outperforms Alternatives

The fiberglass mat reinforcement is the critical differentiator. It bridges hairline cracks in the substrate, distributes impact forces across a wider area (preventing localized delamination), and provides tensile strength that pure epoxy coatings lack. In head-to-head comparisons with FRP panels, FX Wall Armor eliminates every seam — and every seam is a potential failure point for moisture intrusion and bacterial contamination.

For facilities that undergo regular sanitation cycles with quaternary ammonium compounds, peracetic acid, chlorinated alkaline cleaners, or caustic solutions, FX Wall Armor maintains its integrity where paint systems and standard coatings break down. The non-porous finish prevents chemical absorption and allows complete surface sanitization — a requirement for CFIA compliance in food processing and agricultural operations.

Where FX Wall Armor Is Installed

FX Wall Armor is specified in environments where wall surfaces face a combination of mechanical abuse, chemical exposure, and hygiene requirements. Common installations include:

  • Dairy processing rooms and milk receiving areas
  • Meat processing and kill floors
  • Beverage production and packaging lines
  • Commercial kitchens and food preparation areas
  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing clean rooms
  • Agricultural wash bays and robotic milking rooms
  • Wastewater treatment chemical storage areas
  • Manufacturing facilities with forklift traffic near walls

For wall surfaces that need chemical resistance without the structural reinforcement, consider FX Wall-Glaze — a smooth epoxy wall coating designed for lighter-duty sanitation environments. For floor-to-wall transitions, FX Wall Armor pairs with FX Cove to eliminate the 90-degree corner where contamination accumulates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What surfaces can FX Wall Armor be applied to?

FX Wall Armor bonds to concrete block, poured concrete, drywall, and cementitious backer board. The fiberglass mat reinforcement bridges hairline cracks and provides structural integrity across uneven substrates, making it suitable for both new construction and retrofit projects.

Is FX Wall Armor approved for food processing facilities?

Yes. FX Wall Armor creates a seamless, non-porous surface that meets CFIA and FDA requirements for food contact environments. The system resists bacterial harboring, withstands chemical sanitizers and high-pressure wash-downs, and is VOC-compliant for occupied facility application.

How long does FX Wall Armor last?

With proper surface preparation and application, FX Wall Armor delivers 15–20 years of service life in demanding environments. The fiberglass mat reinforcement provides superior impact resistance and prevents delamination, even in facilities with forklift traffic, high-pressure cleaning, and regular chemical exposure.
