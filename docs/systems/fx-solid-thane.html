Your Floors Get Scrubbed Daily. Standard Epoxy Cannot Keep Up.

Facilities that require daily or multiple-daily cleaning cycles — food processing lines, pharmaceutical production areas, commercial kitchens, and laboratory spaces — put enormous stress on floor coatings. Hot water, caustic detergents, acidic sanitizers, and mechanical scrubbing break down conventional epoxy surfaces within a few years. The coating yellows, softens, delaminates at edges, and loses its sanitary properties exactly when you need them most.

FX Solid-Thane is a polyurethane-based floor coating engineered specifically for environments where regular, aggressive cleaning is a daily reality. Its urethane chemistry resists hot water (up to 70 degrees Celsius), caustic and acidic cleaning agents, and the mechanical abrasion of auto-scrubbers and pressure washers. The non-porous, anti-bacterial surface does not absorb moisture, contaminants, or odours — and it stays that way through thousands of wash cycles.

Urethane Chemistry: Why It Matters

Standard epoxy coatings are rigid thermosets. They bond well, they resist many chemicals, and they are cost-effective for moderate-demand environments. But epoxy has inherent limitations: it chalks and yellows under UV exposure, it loses flexibility in thermal cycling, and it softens when exposed to hot water and alkaline cleaning solutions over time.

Polyurethane coatings address all three weaknesses. FX Solid-Thane maintains its colour and gloss under UV exposure, flexes with substrate movement without cracking, and shrugs off hot-water wash-down cycles that would degrade epoxy. The anti-bacterial properties are inherent to the cured film — they are not a surface additive that wears off.

Application and Build-Up

FX Solid-Thane is a thin film system — applied at 8 to 20 mils total dry film thickness over properly prepared concrete. Surface preparation follows SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards with diamond grinding or shot blasting to achieve the required concrete surface profile. An epoxy primer is applied first to bridge the concrete substrate with the urethane topcoat. The Solid-Thane finish coat is then roller-applied in one or two passes depending on the required thickness and performance level.

Despite its thin profile, the urethane film delivers abrasion resistance and chemical durability that matches or exceeds much thicker epoxy-only systems. The thin film build also means faster application and less downtime — most installations are walkable within 12 to 16 hours and fully cured within 48 hours.

Ideal Environments for FX Solid-Thane

  • Food and beverage processing lines where daily sanitization is mandatory
  • Pharmaceutical and nutraceutical production suites requiring GMP-compliant surfaces
  • Commercial and institutional kitchens with grease, hot water, and acidic food exposure
  • Dairy processing facilities with caustic CIP (clean-in-place) wash protocols
  • Brewery and winery production floors exposed to yeast, acids, and hot water
  • Veterinary clinics and animal care facilities requiring hygienic, odour-resistant surfaces

For areas requiring heavier mechanical protection — thermal shock from steam cleaning, impact from dropped heavy loads, or constant forklift traffic — FX Shield provides a thicker polyurethane concrete system. For wall surfaces in the same sanitary environments, FX Wall-Glaze delivers matching cleanability on vertical substrates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes FX Solid-Thane different from standard epoxy?

FX Solid-Thane uses polyurethane chemistry instead of epoxy. This gives it superior flexibility, UV stability, and resistance to hot water and cleaning chemicals. Urethane coatings maintain their appearance and performance under repeated sanitization cycles that would yellow or degrade standard epoxy.

Is FX Solid-Thane suitable for food processing areas?

Yes. FX Solid-Thane's anti-bacterial properties, chemical resistance, and non-porous surface make it well suited for food processing and preparation areas. The system resists common food industry chemicals including caustic wash solutions, acidic byproducts, and sanitizing agents.

How thick is the FX Solid-Thane system?

FX Solid-Thane is a thin film system applied at 8 to 20 mils total dry film thickness. Despite its thin profile, the urethane chemistry delivers excellent abrasion resistance, chemical durability, and anti-slip properties that outperform much thicker epoxy-only systems.
