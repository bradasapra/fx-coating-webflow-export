Your Production Floor Faces Steam Cleaning, Chemical Spills, and Thermal Shock Every Day. Standard Coatings Fail.

Food and beverage processing floors endure conditions that destroy conventional epoxy coatings. Steam cleaning at 80 to 100 degrees Celsius. Caustic wash-down chemicals at concentrations that dissolve organic material. Acidic byproducts from meat, dairy, and fermentation processes. Thermal shock from hot process water hitting cold concrete. Heavy forklift traffic over wet, contaminated surfaces. And all of this happens daily — sometimes multiple times per shift.

Standard epoxy coatings are not engineered for this environment. They soften under heat, delaminate under thermal cycling, and lose adhesion when moisture migrates through the concrete slab. Within two to three years, the coating is cracking, peeling, and becoming a food safety liability rather than a protective asset.

FX Shield is a polyurethane concrete (PU concrete) flooring system — a fundamentally different technology from epoxy. It is applied at 6 to 9 millimetres of thickness (compared to 0.3 to 3 mm for epoxy systems), and it is engineered from the ground up to withstand the thermal shock, chemical exposure, and mechanical abuse that define food and beverage processing environments.

How Polyurethane Concrete Works

Polyurethane concrete combines a polyurethane binder with specially graded aggregates to create a dense, monolithic floor surface. Unlike thin-film coatings that sit on top of concrete, PU concrete becomes part of the floor structure. The system penetrates into the prepared concrete substrate through a specialized primer, and the 6 to 9 mm build creates a new wearing surface with its own structural integrity.

The polyurethane binder gives FX Shield three critical properties that epoxy cannot match:

  • Thermal shock resistance: The system tolerates rapid temperature swings from -40 to +120 degrees Celsius without cracking, delaminating, or losing adhesion. Steam cleaning, hot process water, and cold storage environments are all within its operating range.
  • Flexibility under thermal cycling: PU concrete expands and contracts with the substrate instead of fighting it. This eliminates the micro-cracking that occurs when rigid epoxy coatings are subjected to repeated heating and cooling cycles.
  • Moisture tolerance: FX Shield can be applied to concrete with elevated moisture content and will not delaminate from moisture vapour transmission — the primary failure mode for epoxy coatings on slabs without effective vapour barriers.

Fast Installation, Minimal Downtime

Production downtime is the most expensive part of any flooring project in a food processing facility. FX Shield's chemistry is engineered for rapid cure — the system achieves light foot traffic readiness within 12 to 24 hours and full chemical and mechanical resistance within 48 to 72 hours.

Weekend installations are a standard scheduling approach: surface preparation begins Friday evening after production shuts down, the FX Shield system is applied Saturday, and the floor is ready for full production Monday morning. For larger facilities, phased installations allow production to continue in adjacent areas while coating work proceeds.

The low-odour formulation of FX Shield allows installation without evacuating adjacent production areas — an important consideration for facilities with continuous operations or sensitive inventory nearby.

  • Meat and poultry processing plants — kill floors, cut rooms, and packaging areas
  • Dairy processing facilities — pasteurization areas, cheese production, and CIP rooms

For lighter-duty environments that do not face thermal shock or aggressive chemical exposure, FX Epoxy or FX Quartz provide cost-effective alternatives. For wall protection in the same sanitary environment, pair FX Shield with FX Wall-Glaze and FX Cove for a complete room encapsulation system.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between FX Shield and standard epoxy flooring?

FX Shield uses polyurethane concrete (PU concrete) technology — a fundamentally different chemistry from epoxy. It is applied at 6 to 9 mm thickness compared to epoxy's fraction-of-a-millimetre film. FX Shield withstands thermal shock from -40 to +120 degrees Celsius, resists steam cleaning, and handles the concentrated chemical and mechanical abuse found in food and beverage processing — conditions that would destroy standard epoxy within months.

Is FX Shield CFIA and USDA compliant?

Yes. FX Shield meets the requirements for incidental food contact under USDA and CFIA guidelines. The cured system is non-toxic, non-tainting, and produces no odour transfer to food products. It is specified for meat processing, dairy production, bakeries, brewery floors, and other food and beverage manufacturing environments.

How fast can FX Shield be installed?

FX Shield's polyurethane concrete chemistry enables rapid installation and cure. The system can be applied and returned to light foot traffic within 12 to 24 hours. Full chemical resistance and heavy traffic readiness is achieved within 48 to 72 hours. Weekend installations — Friday evening start with Monday morning production — are a standard scheduling option.
