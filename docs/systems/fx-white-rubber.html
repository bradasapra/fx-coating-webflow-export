The Problem: Aging Roofs Leak, Waste Energy, and Cost a Fortune to Replace

Your facility's roof is aging. Steel panels are corroding at the fastener points. Modified bitumen membranes are cracking and splitting. Galvalume seams have opened from years of thermal expansion and contraction. Every rain event means another bucket on the production floor, another interruption, another emergency repair call. Meanwhile, the dark roofing surface absorbs solar radiation all summer, turning your building into a heat sink and driving cooling costs through the roof — literally.

The conventional answer is a full roof tear-off and replacement: $15–$25 per square foot installed, weeks of disruption, waste disposal costs, and the uncertainty of a major construction project happening above an active production facility. For a 20,000 square foot roof, that is $300,000–$500,000 before the first sheet of new metal goes down.

The Solution: FX White Rubber — Restore Instead of Replace

FX White Rubber is a liquid-applied white synthetic rubber membrane designed as a complete roof restoration system. Applied directly over existing roofing materials — steel, galvalume, modified bitumen, fiberglass, TPO, or EPDM — it creates a seamless, waterproof membrane without the cost, waste, and production disruption of a full replacement. The white reflective finish provides immediate cool roof benefits, reducing surface temperatures and interior cooling loads from the day it cures.

The system is spray-applied or roller-applied in multiple coats, building to a total dry film thickness of 30–50 mils. The synthetic rubber formulation remains permanently flexible, moving with the substrate through thermal cycling without cracking or delaminating. Fastener heads, lap joints, flashing transitions, and penetrations — the typical failure points on any roof — are encapsulated in a continuous membrane that eliminates the seams where leaks originate.

Cool Roof Technology Built In

Unlike FX Black Rubber, which requires a separate FX Cool Roof top coat for UV protection, FX White Rubber delivers waterproofing and solar reflectance in a single system. The bright white finish reflects up to 85% of solar radiation, reducing rooftop surface temperatures by as much as 30°C compared to dark metal or asphalt roofing. For food processing plants, manufacturing facilities, and climate-controlled warehouses, this means measurable reductions in HVAC costs during the June-to-September cooling season.

The integrated UV resistance also means FX White Rubber does not require a separate top coat for longevity. The white rubber membrane itself is the final, exposed surface — engineered to withstand direct sunlight, rain, hail, snow, and freeze-thaw cycling for 15–20 years without chalking, yellowing, or losing its reflective properties.

Ideal for Multi-Substrate Roofs

Many industrial and commercial roofs in Southwestern Ontario are not single-material systems. They have been patched, extended, and modified over decades — sections of standing seam steel next to modified bitumen, with fiberglass skylights and galvalume flashings. Each material expands and contracts at different rates, creating differential movement at the transitions that eventually opens gaps and causes leaks.

FX White Rubber bonds to all these substrates simultaneously. The liquid application conforms to every transition point, and the flexible membrane accommodates the differential movement between materials. One coating system unifies a patchwork roof into a single, seamless waterproof envelope — eliminating the need for multiple specialty products and complicated flashing details.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of roofs can FX White Rubber be applied to?

FX White Rubber adheres to steel, galvalume, galvanized metal, modified bitumen (mod-bit), fiberglass, TPO, EPDM, and previously coated surfaces. It is designed as a restoration system — meaning it can be applied directly over existing roofing materials without tear-off, provided the substrate is structurally sound and properly prepared.

How much can FX White Rubber reduce energy costs?

FX White Rubber reflects up to 85% of solar radiation thanks to its bright white finish, reducing rooftop surface temperatures by as much as 30°C compared to dark roofing materials. For climate-controlled facilities — warehouses, food processing plants, manufacturing floors — this translates to measurable reductions in cooling costs, often 10–25% during peak summer months.

Does FX White Rubber eliminate the need for a full roof replacement?

In most cases, yes. FX White Rubber is a restoration system, not a replacement. It is applied over existing roof materials, sealing leaks, bridging cracks, and creating a new waterproof membrane without the cost, waste, and downtime of a full tear-off and re-roof. The system extends the usable life of an existing roof by 15–20 years.
