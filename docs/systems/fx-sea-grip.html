FX Sea-Grip — Polished Look, Industrial Toughness

Clear polyurethane coating that seals exposed aggregate concrete with a professional wet-look finish, built-in slip resistance, and long-term moisture protection.

The Problem: Beautiful Concrete That Cannot Defend Itself

Exposed aggregate concrete is one of the most visually appealing concrete finishes available — the natural stone aggregate creates depth, texture, and character that no paint or overlay can replicate. But unsealed exposed aggregate is porous. Water penetrates the surface, carrying de-icing salts, oils, and contaminants into the concrete matrix. In Southwestern Ontario's freeze-thaw climate, absorbed moisture expands as it freezes, causing surface spalling, pop-outs, and aggregate loosening. Within a few seasons, that attractive exposed aggregate surface deteriorates into a maintenance liability.

Standard concrete sealers — acrylics, silanes, and siloxanes — provide temporary protection but break down under UV exposure, traffic abrasion, and chemical contact. Acrylic sealers yellow, peel, and require annual reapplication. Penetrating sealers provide moisture resistance but no surface protection against abrasion, staining, or chemical attack. Neither category delivers the combination of durability, appearance enhancement, and slip resistance that commercial and light industrial environments demand.

The Solution: FX Sea-Grip

FX Sea-Grip is a clear, two-component polyurethane coating engineered specifically for sealing and protecting exposed aggregate and decorative concrete surfaces. Unlike commodity sealers that sit on the surface as a thin film, FX Sea-Grip penetrates the concrete pores and builds a durable clear coating on top — typically 6–10 mils dry film thickness — that provides meaningful mechanical protection against traffic wear, chemical exposure, and moisture intrusion.

The polyurethane chemistry delivers superior UV stability compared to epoxy-based clear coats, which tend to yellow and amber over time. FX Sea-Grip maintains its clarity for the full service life of the coating, preserving the natural color and character of the aggregate below. Available in gloss and satin finishes, the coating enhances the concrete's appearance with a subtle wet-look sheen that deepens aggregate colors and adds visual richness without an artificial or "plastic" appearance.

Non-Slip Without Sacrificing Aesthetics

Clear coatings on smooth surfaces create an obvious safety concern — wet sealed concrete can be treacherously slippery. FX Sea-Grip addresses this by incorporating a fine anti-slip aggregate directly into the polyurethane matrix. The result is a micro-textured surface that provides consistent traction in both dry and wet conditions, meeting ASTM D2047 requirements for static coefficient of friction.

The anti-slip texture is deliberately subtle — fine enough to maintain the polished, professional appearance of the sealed aggregate while providing the traction needed for safe pedestrian traffic. This balance of aesthetics and safety makes FX Sea-Grip suitable for spaces where both appearance and liability management matter: building entrances, covered walkways, retail frontages, restaurant patios, brewery taprooms, and recreational facility common areas.

Where FX Sea-Grip Is Specified

FX Sea-Grip is designed for medium-traffic commercial and light industrial environments where the existing concrete substrate is the desired finished surface. Common installations include:

  • Commercial building entrances and covered walkways with exposed aggregate
  • Retail storefront patios and outdoor dining areas
  • Brewery and winery taproom floors
  • Automotive dealership service areas with decorative concrete
  • Recreation centre lobbies and common areas
  • Condominium and apartment building parking structures
  • Light industrial warehouse floors where a clear finish is preferred
  • Agricultural showroom and office floors

For environments that require heavier-duty protection or a pigmented finish rather than a clear seal, FX Epoxy provides a high-build colored coating system. For floors needing aggressive anti-slip texture with a decorative element, FX Quartz Decorative uses colored quartz aggregate for maximum traction and visual impact. For heavy traffic industrial floors, FX Quartz delivers industrial-grade silica-reinforced performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of concrete can FX Sea-Grip be applied to?

FX Sea-Grip is designed for exposed aggregate concrete, broom-finished concrete, stamped concrete, and smooth troweled concrete. It penetrates the surface pores of the concrete and builds a clear protective film on top, enhancing the natural appearance of the aggregate or finish while providing moisture protection and slip resistance. The concrete must be properly cured (minimum 28 days) and free of existing sealers or coatings.

Does FX Sea-Grip change the appearance of the concrete?

FX Sea-Grip enhances the natural appearance of the concrete by deepening the color and adding a subtle wet-look sheen — similar to the appearance of freshly washed concrete. It is available in gloss and satin finishes. The clear formulation does not add color or obscure the aggregate pattern. For facilities that want to add color, FX Epoxy or FX Quartz Decorative provide pigmented alternatives.

How does FX Sea-Grip provide slip resistance on a smooth surface?

FX Sea-Grip incorporates a fine anti-slip aggregate into the polyurethane matrix, creating a micro-textured surface that provides reliable traction in both dry and damp conditions. The texture is subtle enough to maintain the polished aesthetic of the sealed concrete while meeting ASTM D2047 slip resistance requirements. For areas requiring aggressive anti-slip performance, FX Quartz or FX Quartz Decorative offer heavier aggregate textures.
