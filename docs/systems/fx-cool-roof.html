White polyurethane top coat that shields rubber waterproofing membranes from UV degradation while cutting interior temperatures and energy costs.

The Problem: UV Radiation Destroys Waterproofing Membranes

You invested in a waterproofing membrane to protect your grain elevator, storage facility, or industrial building. The membrane stopped the leaks. But now ultraviolet radiation is slowly breaking it down. Every day of direct sun exposure accelerates polymer chain degradation — the membrane becomes chalky, loses elasticity, develops micro-cracks, and eventually fails. In Southwestern Ontario, where summer UV exposure combines with winter freeze-thaw cycling, even premium waterproofing membranes degrade 30–40% faster than manufacturers' lab-tested lifespans suggest.

Without UV protection, an FX Black Rubber membrane on an exposed roof will deliver 10–15 years of service. With UV protection, that same membrane can deliver 20+ years. The math is straightforward — the UV top coat costs a fraction of the underlying waterproofing system, but nearly doubles its effective service life.

The Solution: FX Cool Roof

FX Cool Roof is a white polyurethane coating engineered specifically as a UV-protective top coat for rubber waterproofing membranes. Applied over cured FX Black Rubber, it creates a bright white reflective surface that blocks UV radiation before it reaches the underlying membrane. The polyurethane chemistry provides exceptional UV stability — it does not yellow, chalk, or lose reflectance over time the way acrylic-based "cool" coatings do.

The white finish reflects up to 80% of solar radiation, which delivers two simultaneous benefits. First, it protects the waterproofing membrane from UV-induced degradation, extending membrane life by 10–15 years. Second, it dramatically reduces heat transfer through the roof structure, lowering interior temperatures in unconditioned spaces and cutting HVAC energy consumption in climate-controlled buildings.

How the FX Black Rubber + FX Cool Roof System Works

The standard specification for grain elevator roofs, steel bin tops, and exposed industrial roofing in Southwestern Ontario is a two-layer system:

  1. Base layer — FX Black Rubber: Liquid-applied synthetic rubber membrane at 40–60 mils DFT. Provides the waterproof barrier, crack-bridging flexibility, and corrosion protection. Applied first and allowed to cure for 24–48 hours.
  2. Top coat — FX Cool Roof: White polyurethane at 8–12 mils DFT. Provides UV protection, solar reflectance, and additional weathering resistance. Applied over the cured FX Black Rubber membrane.

Together, the two systems create a total membrane thickness of 48–72 mils — a heavy-duty waterproofing and UV protection assembly that outperforms single-product solutions. The polyurethane top coat also adds abrasion resistance for roofs that receive occasional foot traffic during maintenance.

Energy Savings That Pay for the Coating

For grain storage operations, reducing interior temperatures preserves grain quality by minimizing moisture cycling and condensation. For manufacturing plants and food processing facilities with active HVAC systems, the energy savings from reduced cooling loads often pay for the FX Cool Roof application within 2–4 years. On a 15,000 square foot roof, facility operators typically report 10–25% reductions in summer cooling costs — a return on investment that continues for the full life of the coating.

FX Cool Roof can also be applied as a standalone top coat over other substrates — aged asphalt, modified bitumen, or previously coated surfaces — where the primary goal is solar reflectance and UV protection rather than waterproofing. For facilities that need both waterproofing and UV protection in a single-product system, consider FX White Rubber, which integrates cool roof technology directly into the rubber membrane.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does FX Cool Roof need to be applied at the same time as FX Black Rubber?

No. FX Cool Roof can be applied immediately after FX Black Rubber cures, or it can be added months or even years later as a maintenance top coat. Many facility operators apply FX Black Rubber first for immediate waterproofing, then schedule FX Cool Roof application during a planned maintenance window. The polyurethane bonds chemically to the cured rubber membrane.

How much does FX Cool Roof reduce interior building temperatures?

By reflecting up to 80% of solar radiation, FX Cool Roof reduces rooftop surface temperatures by 25–30°C compared to dark or uncoated surfaces. In practice, this translates to measurable reductions in interior temperatures — typically 5–10°C in unconditioned spaces like grain bins, and 10–25% lower HVAC energy consumption in climate-controlled facilities during peak summer months.

Can FX Cool Roof be recoated?

Yes. FX Cool Roof can be recoated at the end of its service life — typically every 8–12 years — without removing the existing coating or the underlying FX Black Rubber membrane. A maintenance recoat restores full UV reflectance and extends the waterproofing system life by another decade, making it a cost-effective long-term maintenance strategy.
