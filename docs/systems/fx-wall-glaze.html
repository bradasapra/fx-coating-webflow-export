Your Walls Are a Contamination Risk. Painted Block and FRP Panels Are Not Enough.

In food processing, pharmaceutical, and beverage facilities, walls are subject to the same hygiene scrutiny as floors. Painted concrete block absorbs moisture through its porous texture. Paint peels and flakes into production areas. FRP (fiberglass reinforced plastic) panels have seams, joints, and fastener penetrations that harbour bacteria, mould, and moisture behind the panel face. Every seam is a potential audit finding. Every penetration is a contamination pathway.

FX Wall-Glaze eliminates these problems entirely. It is a 100% solids, solventless epoxy coating applied directly to wall surfaces — concrete block, poured concrete, drywall, or properly prepared existing coatings — to create a seamless, non-porous, smooth finish that can be cleaned and sanitized as aggressively as your protocols demand. No seams. No fasteners. No hidden moisture pathways. Just a monolithic, anti-bacterial surface from floor to ceiling.

Zero VOC, Zero Compromise

FX Wall-Glaze is formulated as a 100% solids system with zero volatile organic compounds. This matters for two critical reasons. First, facilities that process food, pharmaceuticals, or sensitive materials cannot tolerate solvent fumes contaminating production areas or inventory. Second, many jurisdictions and facility operators restrict coating work to periods when the building is unoccupied — the zero-VOC formulation of FX Wall-Glaze allows installation in active facilities without evacuation or extended shutdowns.

The solventless formulation also means the coating cures to a harder, denser film than solvent-based alternatives. There is no solvent evaporation creating micro-porosity in the cured film. The result is a genuinely non-porous surface that resists moisture penetration, chemical absorption, and bacterial colonization from day one.

Application Process

Wall surfaces are prepared by cleaning, degreasing, and mechanically profiling where necessary. Concrete block walls are filled with a cementitious block filler to create a smooth substrate, then primed with a penetrating epoxy primer. The FX Wall-Glaze finish coat is applied by roller or spray in one or two coats at 10 to 20 mils dry film thickness per coat. The result is a smooth, glossy, seamless surface that looks and performs like glass — but with the toughness and chemical resistance of high-performance epoxy.

FX Wall-Glaze pairs naturally with FX Cove at the wall-to-floor transition. The cove base creates a seamless, radiused transition that eliminates the sharp corner where walls meet floors — the single most common location for bacterial accumulation and cleaning difficulty in sanitary facilities.
