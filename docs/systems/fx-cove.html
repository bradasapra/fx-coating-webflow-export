Rolled Radius Coving for Seamless, Sanitary Floor-to-Wall Transitions

The Corner Where Your Floor Meets the Wall Is Your Facility's Biggest Hygiene Weakness.

Every facility with wash-down cleaning protocols knows the problem. The 90-degree angle where the floor slab meets the wall is almost impossible to clean properly. Mop heads cannot reach into the corner. Squeegees push water and residue along the junction without removing it. Pressure washers drive contaminants deeper into the joint. Over time, bacteria, mould, chemical residue, and organic material accumulate in that sharp corner — invisible to the eye but detectable in swab tests and facility audits.

This is not a maintenance failure. It is a design failure. And it has a straightforward engineering solution.

FX Cove is a rolled radius coving system that replaces the sharp 90-degree floor-to-wall junction with a smooth, radiused curve. The cove base is formed from epoxy or urethane mortar, shaped to a consistent radius profile, and coated with the same material as the floor and wall surfaces. The result is a continuous, seamless transition that cleaning equipment and wash-down water flow over naturally — no corners for contaminants to hide in, no joints for moisture to penetrate.

How FX Cove Is Installed

The cove base is formed after the floor coating is applied and before wall coatings go on. This sequencing ensures the cove integrates seamlessly with both surfaces. The installation process follows these steps:

  1. The floor-to-wall junction is cleaned and any existing sealant, caulk, or deteriorated material is removed
  2. An epoxy or urethane mortar is mixed and applied to the junction using a cove trowel or roller tool
  3. The mortar is shaped to the specified radius profile — typically 1 inch (25 mm), 2 inches (50 mm), or 4 inches (100 mm)
  4. After initial cure, the cove base is sanded smooth and coated with the same system as the adjacent floor and wall
  5. The finished cove creates a monolithic, seamless surface from floor to wall with no joints, seams, or transitions

The entire process adds minimal time to the overall floor and wall coating project — typically one day for the cove installation plus one day for the topcoat. But the hygiene benefit is permanent and measurable.

Why Radius Matters

Regulatory standards for food processing, pharmaceutical, and dairy facilities increasingly specify cove base at floor-to-wall junctions. CFIA (Canadian Food Inspection Agency) guidelines and USDA sanitary design principles both recommend eliminating sharp corners in production areas. The radius profile is not decorative — it is a functional requirement for facilities that must demonstrate sanitary design during audits and inspections.

The 4-inch (100 mm) radius is the most commonly specified profile for food and dairy processing facilities. It provides sufficient curvature for auto-scrubbers, foam cleaning systems, and high-pressure wash-down equipment to clean the transition completely in a single pass. Smaller radii (1-inch and 2-inch) are appropriate for corridors, storage areas, and spaces with less aggressive cleaning protocols.

System Integration

FX Cove is not a standalone product — it is a critical component of a complete sanitary room system. It integrates with:

  • FX Epoxy and FX Quartz floor systems for standard duty environments
  • FX Solid-Thane for urethane-topped floors in high-frequency cleaning environments
  • FX Shield for heavy-duty polyurethane concrete floors in food and beverage processing
  • FX Wall-Glaze for seamless wall surfaces above the cove transition

When FX Cove is installed with matching floor and wall coatings, the entire room — floor, cove, walls — becomes a monolithic, sealed enclosure with no joints, penetrations, or sharp corners. This is the gold standard for sanitary facility design.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is cove base important in food processing facilities?

The 90-degree junction where floors meet walls is the most difficult area to clean in any facility. Dirt, bacteria, moisture, and chemical residue accumulate in sharp corners and resist standard cleaning equipment. FX Cove replaces that sharp angle with a smooth, radiused curve that cleaning equipment and wash-down water can flow over without obstruction, eliminating the primary harbour point for contaminants.

What radius options are available for FX Cove?

FX Cove is available in standard radius profiles of 1 inch (25 mm), 2 inches (50 mm), and 4 inches (100 mm). The 4-inch radius is most commonly specified for food processing, dairy, and pharmaceutical facilities where aggressive wash-down protocols require maximum cleanability at the floor-to-wall transition.

Can FX Cove be installed with any floor coating system?

Yes. FX Cove is designed to integrate seamlessly with all FX floor coating systems including FX Epoxy, FX Quartz, FX Solid-Thane, and FX Shield. The cove base is installed after the floor coating and before the wall coating, creating a continuous, sealed transition from floor surface to wall surface.
