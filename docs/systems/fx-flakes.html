Bio-secure, hygienic, and slip-safe decorative flooring that transforms bare concrete into a professional-grade surface — without sacrificing durability or cleanability.

The Problem: Your Floor Needs to Look Professional and Perform Under Pressure

Not every facility floor exists behind closed doors. Veterinary clinics, automotive dealerships, retail showrooms, commercial kitchens, pharmaceutical dispensaries, recreational facilities, and office lobbies all share a common challenge — the floor is visible to clients, customers, and inspectors. It needs to look clean, professional, and intentional. But it also needs to handle foot traffic, rolling loads, spills, chemical cleaning, and daily wear without showing every scuff and stain.

Polished concrete looks great on day one but shows every oil stain and tire mark within weeks. Tile grout lines harbor bacteria and require constant maintenance. VCT and sheet vinyl tear, discolor, and need frequent replacement. Bare painted concrete chips and peels. These are floors that either look good or perform well — rarely both.

The Solution: FX Flakes

FX Flakes is a multi-layer decorative flooring system that combines the industrial durability of epoxy with the visual sophistication of a custom-designed finish. The system consists of a high-build epoxy base coat, a full or partial broadcast of decorative vinyl flake chips, and a clear protective top coat — either polyurethane for standard environments or polyaspartic for rapid-cure applications.

The vinyl flake broadcast creates a natural granite-like or terrazzo-inspired pattern that actively disguises minor imperfections in the substrate, hides everyday dust and light soiling, and provides consistent visual texture across the entire floor area. The result is a floor that looks cleaner between maintenance cycles and maintains its professional appearance for years — not months.

Built-In Safety and Hygiene

FX Flakes is not purely cosmetic. The flake broadcast creates a micro-textured surface profile that provides reliable slip resistance in both dry and damp conditions, meeting ASTM D2047 requirements for static coefficient of friction. In facilities where spills are inevitable — veterinary clinics, commercial kitchens, brewery taprooms, car wash bays — FX Flakes provides traction without resorting to aggressive anti-slip textures that are difficult to clean.

The sealed top coat creates a non-porous surface that resists bacterial colonization, chemical staining, and moisture absorption. This makes FX Flakes a practical choice for environments governed by hygiene standards — veterinary examination rooms, pharmaceutical compounding areas, food service operations, and healthcare waiting areas. The seamless installation eliminates grout lines and seams where bacteria accumulate, and the surface can be sanitized with standard commercial cleaning products without degradation.

Full Broadcast vs. Partial Broadcast

FX Flakes is available in two application densities:

  • Full broadcast (chip-to-rejection): Vinyl flakes are broadcast to full saturation, completely covering the epoxy base coat. The result is a dense, uniform chip pattern with maximum texture and visual impact. Ideal for showrooms, lobbies, and high-visibility areas where aesthetics are the primary driver.
  • Partial broadcast: Flakes are scattered at reduced density over the epoxy base, allowing the base coat color to show through between chips. This creates a lighter, more subtle pattern with a smoother overall texture. Suitable for facilities where cleanability is prioritized over maximum visual impact.

Both densities receive the same protective top coat and deliver equivalent durability and chemical resistance. The choice between full and partial broadcast is primarily aesthetic and driven by the facility's visual requirements.

Color Customization

With over 40 standard color blends and the option for custom combinations, FX Flakes can be matched to corporate branding, facility design schemes, or safety zone designations. Common selections include neutral earth tones for professional offices, warm sand and granite patterns for retail environments, and high-contrast combinations for safety demarcation in manufacturing facilities. Flake chip sizes range from fine 1/16-inch to dramatic 1/4-inch, allowing further customization of the finished appearance.

For facilities that need anti-slip performance with a decorative quartz aggregate instead of vinyl flakes, consider FX Quartz Decorative. For environments requiring maximum chemical and mechanical resistance without the decorative element, FX Epoxy or FX Quartz provide the industrial-grade performance needed in heavy-duty production areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What color options are available for FX Flakes?

FX Flakes is available in over 40 standard color blends, ranging from neutral earth tones and granite patterns to bold multi-color combinations. Custom blends can be created to match corporate branding, facility color schemes, or safety zone designations. The vinyl flake chips come in multiple sizes — from fine 1/16-inch chips for subtle texture to larger 1/4-inch chips for dramatic visual effect.

Is FX Flakes suitable for areas with heavy foot traffic?

Yes. FX Flakes is engineered for medium to heavy foot traffic, rolling cart loads, and light wheeled equipment. The multi-layer system — epoxy base, vinyl flake broadcast, and polyurethane or polyaspartic top coat — creates a durable composite that resists wear, abrasion, and point loading. For environments with heavy forklift traffic or extreme mechanical abuse, FX Shield or FX Quartz may be more appropriate.

How long does it take to install FX Flakes?

A typical FX Flakes installation — including surface preparation, epoxy base coat, flake broadcast, and top coat — takes 2–3 days for a 2,000–5,000 square foot area. Light foot traffic is possible within 24 hours of the final top coat. Full chemical cure takes 5–7 days. FX Coating coordinates installation schedules to minimize operational disruption.
