FX Quartz Decorative — Eye-Catching Anti-Slip Flooring

Decorative quartz aggregate flooring that delivers visual impact and slip-safe performance in one VOC-compliant, low-odour system — built for high-traffic commercial and institutional spaces.

The Problem: High-Traffic Floors That Must Look Good and Stay Safe

Hospital corridors. School hallways. Retail store floors. Office building lobbies. Recreation centre change rooms. These are surfaces that see hundreds or thousands of footfalls daily, encounter wet conditions regularly, and are visible to every person who walks through the door. The floor is often the largest single visual element in the space — and when it looks worn, stained, or neglected, the entire facility looks unprofessional.

Traditional flooring options force a compromise. VCT (vinyl composition tile) requires constant waxing and stripping — a labor-intensive cycle that adds up to thousands of dollars annually. Ceramic tile provides durability but the grout lines stain, crack, and harbor bacteria. Polished concrete looks modern but offers poor slip resistance when wet. Carpet is warm but impossible to sanitize. Every option either looks good temporarily and demands constant maintenance, or performs well but lacks the visual refinement that public-facing environments require.

The Solution: FX Quartz Decorative

FX Quartz Decorative is a multi-layer epoxy flooring system that uses colored decorative quartz aggregate to create a visually striking, naturally anti-slip surface. The system begins with a properly prepared concrete substrate per SSPC-SP13/NACE 6, followed by a high-build epoxy base coat, a full broadcast of colored quartz aggregate, and a clear polyurethane or polyaspartic top coat that locks the aggregate in place and provides chemical resistance, UV stability, and easy cleanability.

The colored quartz aggregate is the distinguishing element. Available in dozens of color blends — from warm sandstones and cool grays to vibrant multi-tone combinations — the quartz creates a natural stone appearance with depth and visual complexity that flat-color coatings cannot achieve. Each grain of quartz is individually colored and coated, meaning the floor maintains its color throughout the wear layer, not just on the surface. The aggregate also provides inherent slip resistance — the natural texture of the quartz grains creates micro-scale traction that exceeds ASTM D2047 requirements without additional anti-slip additives.

Low Odour, VOC-Compliant — Designed for Occupied Spaces

One of the critical advantages of FX Quartz Decorative over competing systems is its formulation for occupied-building installation. The low-odour, VOC-compliant resin system allows application in hospitals, schools, retail stores, and office buildings without evacuating the building or shutting down adjacent operations. This is not a secondary consideration — it is often the deciding factor for facility managers who cannot afford multi-day shutdowns for floor renovation.

The rapid-cure polyaspartic top coat option further reduces downtime. A floor that is prepared, coated, and broadcast in the morning can receive its top coat the same afternoon and be open to light foot traffic the next morning — a 24-hour turnaround that traditional epoxy systems with 72-hour cure times cannot match.

Eliminating Ongoing Maintenance Costs

FX Quartz Decorative is a zero-wax, zero-strip flooring system. The sealed polyurethane top coat eliminates the need for the wax-strip-buff cycle that VCT demands every 4–6 weeks. Daily dust mopping and periodic wet mopping with a neutral pH cleaner is the full extent of required maintenance. Over a 10–15 year service life, this maintenance reduction typically saves $2–$4 per square foot compared to VCT — which means FX Quartz Decorative often costs less than VCT over the life of the floor, despite a higher initial installation cost.

For facilities that prefer the look of vinyl flake chips over quartz aggregate, FX Flakes provides a similar decorative-plus-performance combination. For heavy industrial environments where aesthetics are secondary to maximum chemical and abrasion resistance, standard FX Quartz uses industrial-grade silica for unmatched mechanical performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does FX Quartz Decorative differ from standard FX Quartz?

Standard FX Quartz uses industrial-grade silica quartz for maximum anti-slip performance and chemical resistance in heavy-duty production environments. FX Quartz Decorative uses colored decorative quartz aggregate that delivers the same anti-slip benefits but with a visually refined, multi-toned finish suitable for client-facing and public-access spaces. Both systems use the same epoxy base and top coat technology.

Can FX Quartz Decorative be installed in occupied buildings?

Yes. FX Quartz Decorative is formulated with low-odour, VOC-compliant resins specifically to allow installation in occupied commercial and institutional buildings. The system does not require building evacuation during application, making it practical for phased renovations in hospitals, schools, retail stores, and office buildings where operations continue during installation.

What maintenance does FX Quartz Decorative require?

FX Quartz Decorative requires minimal maintenance. Daily dust mopping and periodic wet mopping with a neutral pH cleaner keeps the surface in optimal condition. The sealed top coat prevents staining and eliminates the need for waxing, stripping, or polishing — a significant long-term maintenance cost reduction compared to VCT, tile, or polished concrete floors.
