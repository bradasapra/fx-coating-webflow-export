The People Behind Every FX Coating Project

Every coating system is only as good as the crew that prepares the surface and applies it. At FX Coating Ltd., our team members are trained specialists — not general labourers handed a roller. Each person on our crew understands substrate assessment, surface preparation standards, resin chemistry, and the application techniques required to achieve a coating system's full performance potential.

Our applicators hold practical supervisor training certifications and work to SSPC/NACE surface preparation standards on every project. They understand the difference between shot blasting and scarification, know when moisture testing indicates a vapour barrier issue, and can identify substrate defects that would cause premature coating failure if left unaddressed.

This level of technical knowledge is what separates a coating that lasts 3 years from one that lasts 20. It is also why facility managers who have worked with us once tend to call us back for their next project.

Training and Professional Standards

Industrial coating application is a skilled trade. The difference between a coating that performs for two decades and one that fails within a year often comes down to preparation technique, mixing ratios, application thickness, and environmental conditions during cure. Our team maintains current knowledge and training in:

  • SSPC/NACE Surface Preparation Standards — including SP-13/NACE 6 for concrete surface preparation, the standard required for high-performance coating adhesion
  • Practical Supervisor Training — ensuring on-site leadership understands both the technical requirements and the safety protocols for every project
  • Manufacturer Certification Programs — our applicators are trained and certified by the manufacturers whose products form the basis of our FX coating systems
  • Health and Safety Compliance — COR certification, WSIB coverage, and documented safety procedures for confined space entry, respiratory protection, and chemical handling
  • CFIA/FDA Compliance Knowledge — understanding the regulatory requirements for coatings installed in food processing and food contact environments

We invest in our team's development because our clients' facilities depend on it. A food processing plant floor that fails inspection because of improper coating application costs the facility manager far more than the original coating project. A wastewater containment lining that delaminates because of inadequate surface preparation creates an environmental liability. We train to prevent these outcomes.

Learn more about our safety and quality certifications, or contact our team to discuss your project requirements.
