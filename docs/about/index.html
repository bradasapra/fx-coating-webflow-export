Industrial Coating Expertise Built Over Two Decades

FX Coating Ltd. is a family-owned and operated industrial coating contractor based in Southwestern Ontario. Since 2005, we have provided turn-key surface modification services to facilities across the region — from grain elevators and food processing plants to wastewater treatment facilities and manufacturing operations.

Our focus is straightforward: protect your facility's concrete, steel, and structural surfaces so they perform longer, clean easier, and meet the regulatory standards your industry demands. We do this with proprietary coating systems developed for the specific conditions found in Ontario's agricultural, food processing, industrial, and institutional facilities.

Every project begins with a detailed site assessment. We evaluate your substrate conditions, chemical exposure, traffic patterns, thermal environment, and regulatory requirements before recommending a system. This approach means the coating we install is engineered for your specific operating conditions — not a generic product pulled off a shelf.

What Sets FX Coating Apart

Many coating contractors install whatever product their distributor stocks. FX Coating operates differently. We developed our own branded product lines — FX Shield, FX Epoxy, FX Quartz, FX Black Rubber, and others — because no single manufacturer's catalogue covered the range of conditions our clients face.

Each FX system is formulated for a specific set of demands: thermal shock resistance for food processing floors, chemical containment for wastewater facilities, impact and abrasion protection for manufacturing plants, and moisture-resistant coatings for grain elevators and agricultural operations.

We handle every phase of the project in-house: substrate assessment, surface preparation to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards, coating application, and final inspection. No subcontractors on the critical path. One crew, one point of accountability, from start to finish.
