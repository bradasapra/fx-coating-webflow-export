Your Facility Demands More Than a Contractor Who Shows Up With a Roller

Industrial coating projects happen in operating facilities — food processing plants with active production lines, wastewater treatment facilities with confined spaces, grain elevators with explosive dust atmospheres, and manufacturing floors where heavy equipment operates daily. These environments require a contractor with documented safety credentials, auditable quality control procedures, and the regulatory knowledge to ensure the installed system meets your industry's compliance standards.

FX Coating Ltd. maintains a comprehensive safety and quality program because our clients' facilities demand it. Procurement teams require COR certification before we can bid. Insurance carriers require WSIB clearance certificates. Food processing plant managers require documented proof that our systems meet CFIA and FDA standards. We do not treat these as paperwork — they are the operational standards that govern how we work on every project.

Our Certifications and Compliance Standards

SSPC-SP13 / NACE 6

Every FX Coating project begins with surface preparation to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards — the industry benchmark for concrete substrate preparation prior to coating application. This standard specifies the mechanical surface preparation methods (shot blasting, scarification, grinding) required to achieve proper adhesion between the coating system and the concrete substrate. Without proper SP-13 preparation, even the best coating system will fail prematurely.

COR Certification

FX Coating holds a Certificate of Recognition (COR) — the nationally recognized safety accreditation for Canadian contractors. COR certification requires a documented health and safety management system that meets or exceeds provincial requirements, verified through independent audit. Many facility owners and general contractors require COR certification as a prerequisite for contractor pre-qualification.

WSIB Coverage

FX Coating maintains full Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB) coverage with current clearance certificates available upon request. WSIB compliance protects our clients from liability exposure and ensures that our workers are covered under Ontario's workplace safety insurance system. We provide updated clearance certificates with every project proposal.

CFIA Compliant Systems

Our coating systems for food processing facilities meet Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) requirements for surfaces in food production environments. Systems like FX Shield and FX Wall-Glaze are formulated for incidental food contact, produce no odour transfer, and create seamless, cleanable surfaces that meet sanitary design principles.

FDA Approved Systems

For facilities that export to the United States or operate under FDA regulatory frameworks, FX Coating provides coating systems that meet FDA requirements for food contact surfaces. Our polyurethane concrete and epoxy systems are formulated with FDA-listed components and produce non-toxic, non-tainting cured surfaces suitable for food and beverage processing environments.

OMAFRA Compliance

Ontario Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (OMAFRA) standards govern facility requirements for agricultural operations including dairy barns, milking parlours, livestock housing, and grain storage. FX Coating systems meet OMAFRA requirements for durable, cleanable, moisture-resistant surfaces in agricultural facilities across Southwestern Ontario.

GFSI Standards

Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI) benchmarked standards — including SQF, BRC, and FSSC 22000 — require that food processing facility surfaces be constructed of materials that are durable, cleanable, and resistant to the chemicals used in sanitation programs. FX Coating systems are specified to support GFSI audit compliance for floor, wall, and cove surfaces in food manufacturing environments.

Manufacturer Certifications

Our applicators are trained and certified by the resin and material manufacturers whose products form the basis of the FX coating systems. This includes Tnemec certified applicator status and training from other leading industrial coating manufacturers. Manufacturer certification ensures that our application methods match the product specifications — protecting both system performance and warranty coverage.

Full Insurance Coverage

FX Coating carries comprehensive general liability insurance and professional liability coverage appropriate for industrial coating work. Insurance certificates are provided with every project proposal and are available for review during contractor pre-qualification. Our coverage levels meet or exceed the requirements of major industrial, institutional, and food processing facility owners across Ontario.

How FX Coating Ensures Quality on Every Project

Quality in industrial coatings is not a slogan — it is a documented process with measurable checkpoints at every phase. A coating failure in a food processing plant can shut down production. A lining failure in a wastewater containment structure can create an environmental incident. We eliminate these risks through a four-phase quality control process that governs every project we undertake.

1. Site Assessment and System Specification

Every project begins with a detailed on-site assessment. We evaluate the existing substrate condition, moisture content, chemical exposure profile, traffic patterns, thermal environment, and regulatory requirements specific to your facility. This assessment drives the system specification — we do not recommend products until we understand the conditions they need to survive. The result is a written specification that documents the recommended system, surface preparation method, application procedure, and expected performance parameters.

2. Surface Preparation to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6

Surface preparation is the most critical phase of any coating project. We prepare all concrete substrates to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards using mechanical methods — shot blasting, scarification, or diamond grinding depending on substrate condition and coating system requirements. We verify surface profile, moisture content, and contamination levels before proceeding to coating application. If the substrate does not meet specification, we address the deficiency before moving forward. Coating over an improperly prepared surface is not an option.

3. Coating Application and Environmental Monitoring

During application, we monitor and document ambient temperature, humidity, substrate temperature, and dew point — all factors that affect coating adhesion and cure. We verify wet film thickness during application and dry film thickness after cure to confirm that the installed system meets the manufacturer's specification. Mixing ratios, pot life, and recoat windows are tracked to ensure proper resin chemistry throughout the application process.

4. Final Inspection and Documentation

Upon completion, we conduct a final inspection covering film thickness, adhesion, appearance, and overall system integrity. We document the completed installation with photographs and provide the client with a project completion report that includes the system specification, surface preparation methods used, application conditions, and thickness measurements. This documentation supports your facility's compliance records and provides a baseline for future maintenance planning.

Job Site Safety Protocols

Industrial coating work involves chemical handling, confined space entry, respiratory protection, and operation of mechanical surface preparation equipment. Our COR-certified safety program addresses each of these hazards with documented procedures, required training, and on-site compliance verification.

  • Confined Space Entry: Documented entry procedures, atmospheric monitoring, and standby rescue personnel for tank linings, pit coatings, and enclosed areas
  • Respiratory Protection: Fit-tested respirators and supplied air systems for solvent-based and two-component coating application in enclosed environments
  • Chemical Handling: Material Safety Data Sheets on-site for all products, proper storage and disposal procedures, and spill response protocols
  • Fall Protection: Harness and anchor systems for elevated work including tank interiors, elevated platforms, and roofing applications
  • Hot Work Controls: Permits and fire watch procedures when grinding, cutting, or welding is required during surface preparation
  • Daily Toolbox Talks: Crew safety briefings before work begins each day, covering site-specific hazards, task-specific risks, and emergency procedures

Our safety record is available upon request during contractor pre-qualification. We work with facility safety coordinators to integrate our procedures with your site-specific safety requirements, including lockout/tagout coordination, traffic management, and production schedule alignment.
