From Agricultural Roots to Full-Service Industrial Coatings

FX Coating Ltd. was founded in 2005 as a family-owned business in Southwestern Ontario. The company started with a clear objective: provide reliable, long-lasting surface protection for the agricultural facilities that form the economic backbone of the region. Grain elevators, dairy barns, livestock operations, and feed processing plants — these were the first facilities we protected, and they taught us everything about what coatings need to survive in real working environments.

Those early years were defined by hands-on problem solving. Agricultural facilities do not operate in controlled conditions. Concrete floors absorb moisture from animals and wash-down cycles. Walls take impact from equipment and livestock. Grain dust, ammonia, acidic silage runoff, and constant thermal cycling between heated interiors and sub-zero Ontario winters create an environment that destroys ordinary paint and cheap epoxy within months. We learned quickly that the standard products available through most distributors were not engineered for these conditions.

Developing the FX Product Lines

Rather than continue applying products that failed our clients within a few years, we began developing our own systems. Working directly with resin manufacturers and aggregate suppliers, we formulated coatings specifically for the conditions found in Ontario's agricultural and food processing facilities. These became the FX product lines — FX Shield, FX Epoxy, FX Quartz, FX Black Rubber, FX Wall-Glaze, and others — each engineered for a specific set of performance requirements.

FX Shield, our polyurethane concrete system, was developed because no off-the-shelf coating could survive the thermal shock and chemical exposure in food processing plants. FX Black Rubber came from the need for flexible, chemical-resistant linings in wastewater containment and secondary containment applications. FX Wall-Glaze was our answer to facility managers who needed wall surfaces that could be pressure-washed and sanitized without degradation.

Each system exists because a client had a real problem that existing products could not solve. We do not add products to our line for marketing purposes — every FX system addresses a documented performance gap.

Growing Into New Industries

As our reputation for durable, specification-grade work grew within the agricultural sector, facility managers in other industries began reaching out. A food processing plant manager who had seen our work in a grain elevator contacted us about their production floor. A municipal engineer who had heard about our tank lining work asked us to quote a wastewater treatment facility. A manufacturing plant operations director needed chemical-resistant flooring for their production area.

Each new industry brought its own set of challenges and regulatory requirements. Food processing facilities required CFIA and FDA-compliant systems with documented food safety credentials. Wastewater treatment plants needed coatings that could withstand hydrogen sulphide, caustic chemicals, and constant immersion. Manufacturing operations demanded abrasion-resistant floors that could handle heavy forklift traffic, chemical spills, and impact loading.

We met each challenge by adapting our systems and developing new ones. Our team invested in training — SSPC and NACE surface preparation standards, manufacturer certification programs, and health and safety credentials including COR certification and full WSIB compliance. We did not simply expand our sales territory; we expanded our technical capability to match the demands of each new market.

Our Philosophy

FX Coating Ltd. offers integral services to companies looking for growth and transformation. Our focus into building authentic product lines offers our clients increased efficiency and less workload problems. That philosophy has guided every decision since 2005 — from the systems we develop, to the preparation standards we follow, to the way we structure our project timelines around our clients' production schedules.

We believe that a coating system is only as good as the surface preparation beneath it. That is why every FX Coating project begins with substrate assessment and surface preparation to SSPC-SP13/NACE 6 standards. Proper preparation is not optional — it is the foundation that determines whether a coating lasts 3 years or 20 years.

We believe in accountability. One crew handles your project from assessment through final inspection. No subcontractors on the critical path. If something goes wrong, there is one phone number to call and one company standing behind the work.

Serving Southwestern Ontario

Today, FX Coating Ltd. serves more than 12 communities across Southwestern Ontario, including London, Kitchener-Waterloo, Hamilton, Guelph, Woodstock, Stratford, St. Thomas, Chatham-Kent, Sarnia, Windsor, Cambridge, and Brantford. Our service area covers the region's highest concentration of agricultural operations, food processing plants, manufacturing facilities, and municipal infrastructure.

We remain family-owned and operated. The same values that guided our first grain elevator project in 2005 — do the preparation right, use the right system for the conditions, stand behind the work — guide every project we take on today.
