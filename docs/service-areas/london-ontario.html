London Is Where FX Coating Started — and Where We Know Every Facility Corridor

London, Ontario is the home base of FX Coating Ltd. Our crews, our equipment, and our project management team all operate from here — which means London-area facilities get the fastest mobilization, the shortest travel overhead, and the most direct access to our senior applicators. When a London facility needs emergency coating repair on a Friday afternoon, we are not driving two hours to get there.

London's industrial landscape has shaped our expertise. The city's manufacturing sector — concentrated along Veteran's Memorial Parkway, Innovation Park, and the Huron Industrial Area — generates steady demand for high-performance floor coatings that withstand forklift traffic, chemical exposure, and continuous shift operations. We have coated warehouse floors, machine shop bays, and assembly areas across these industrial parks, and we understand the scheduling constraints that come with 24/7 manufacturing operations.

The food processing sector in London and surrounding Middlesex County is another core focus. From meat processing plants to dairy operations to commercial bakeries, these facilities require CFIA-compliant floor and wall coatings that resist thermal shock from steam cleaning, hold up against organic acids and caustic wash-down chemicals, and maintain sanitary surfaces that pass regulatory inspections. Our FX Shield polyurethane concrete system and FX Wall-Glaze are specified repeatedly in these environments.

Agricultural operations in the surrounding Middlesex County townships — dairy farms with robotic milking rooms, grain storage facilities, livestock operations — rely on coating systems that perform in wet, corrosive conditions with minimal maintenance. FX Epoxy and FX Quartz provide durable, easy-clean surfaces for these demanding agricultural environments.

London's institutional sector also benefits from our proximity. Municipal buildings, fire stations, recreation centres, healthcare facilities, and Western University campus buildings all require coating solutions that balance durability with aesthetics. Our FX Flakes and FX Quartz Decorative systems deliver the visual appeal these spaces require while maintaining the abrasion and chemical resistance needed for high-traffic public environments.
