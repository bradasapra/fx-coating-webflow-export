Serving Southwestern Ontario from Our London Home Base

FX Coating Ltd. is headquartered in London, Ontario, at the centre of Southwestern Ontario's industrial and agricultural corridor. From this strategic location, our crews deploy across the region to deliver epoxy flooring, protective coatings, concrete surface preparation, and tank and vessel linings for facilities of every scale — from single-room dairy operations to multi-building manufacturing complexes.

Our regional focus means shorter travel times, faster project mobilization, and crews who understand the local industries. Whether your facility processes grain in Chatham-Kent, manufactures automotive parts in Kitchener-Waterloo, or operates a food production line in Hamilton, FX Coating has the proximity and the expertise to get your project completed on schedule.

Rapid Response Across the Region

Every facility in Southwestern Ontario is within a two-hour drive of our London headquarters. For urgent coating repairs — containment breaches, failed flooring in active production areas, or emergency waterproofing — our crews can mobilize same-day to assess the situation and begin remediation. Scheduled projects benefit from reduced travel costs and the ability to phase work around your production calendar without extended crew accommodation expenses.
