Schefter Poultry — Wall Lining Restoration

The walls in this facility were hard to clean, prone to flaking, and the many crevices provided spaces for bacteria to grow—making it a costly and time-consuming process to keep them up to code.

We lined the entire wall with a non-porous reinforced coating that sealed off cracks and crevices where bacteria could hide, then applied a specialized non-stick finish designed to prevent buildup and simplify cleaning. This dual-layer system created a seamless, hygienic wall surface, strengthened durability, and cut cleaning time in half.
