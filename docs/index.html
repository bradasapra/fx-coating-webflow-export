Biosecure Flooring for Poultry Processing

Scott Helps of Helps Poultry faced a challenge familiar to every commercial poultry operation: maintaining strict biosecurity standards in high-traffic egg processing areas. The egg sorting room and cooler needed flooring that could withstand constant washing, resist bacterial growth, and provide slip resistance in wet conditions.

By choosing FX Coating's specialized solutions, he transformed potential compliance risks into competitive advantages. The egg sorting area received a decorative flake broadcast system with wall-to-floor transitions designed for complete washdown capability, while the cooler room got a thermal-resistant epoxy/urethane system built to handle temperature cycling.
