Protective Coating

Defending Your Critical Infrastructure with Durable Protective Coatings

Extend Surface Life, Minimize Downtime, Maximize Safety

Tough environments demand tougher surfaces. Our coatings extend the life of concrete and steel across Ontario’s most demanding industries — from food plants and pharmaceutical labs to agri-facilities and manufacturing floors. Get corrosion-resistant coatings for metal, antimicrobial urethane cements for wet, high-traffic zones, and seamless epoxy or MMA systems that balance durability with aesthetics. For walls, trenches, and curbs, our fibreglass reinforcements and patching systems deliver non-porous, impact-resistant finishes that withstand heavy abuse and harsh chemicals. Every coating is built to last, built for your industry, and built to protect people and productivity.

Who Needs Protective Coatings?

Food and beverage processors requiring CFIA-compliant surfaces
Facilities with floors or walls exposed to moisture, heat, or chemicals
Maintenance managers repairing damaged coatings or preventing rust
Producers seeking durable, easy-to-clean, washdown-safe surfaces
Plants looking to extend asset life with minimal shutdown
